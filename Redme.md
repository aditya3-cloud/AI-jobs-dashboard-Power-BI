# 🚀 AI Jobs Market Dashboard (Power BI)

## 📊 Overview

This project analyzes the impact of AI on job roles, salary trends, and skill demand using an interactive Power BI dashboard.

## 🔍 Key Insights

* ML Engineer & Cloud Engineer are highest paying roles (~$48K)
* Medium AI-risk jobs dominate (~48%)
* Clear relationship between AI risk and salary trends

## 🛠 Tools Used

* Power BI
* DAX
* Data Cleaning
* Data Visualization

## 📌 Features

* Interactive filters (Country, Job Role, Experience, Year)
* KPI Cards (Salary, Job Openings, AI Risk, Skill Demand)
* AI Risk vs Salary Scatter Plot
* Job Distribution by AI Risk (Donut Chart)
* Global Job Openings Heatmap
